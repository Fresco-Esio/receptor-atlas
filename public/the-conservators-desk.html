This desk holds the accuracy review for the three volumes of the Atlas — The Receptor Cabinet, The Clinical Ledger, and The Receptor Archive. Each of the 24 receptor specimens is examined against four checks, cross-referenced to a specific locus in Stahl’s Essential Psychopharmacology (5th ed., 2021) and a peer-reviewed source, and rated for your own command of the material.

Work the halls in order — dopamine first, then serotonin, noradrenaline, glutamate & GABA, and the neuromodulators. The sequence builds the conceptual scaffolding the way the systems build on one another. Progress is saved in this browser automatically; export a copy regularly.

The review protocol — what each pass examines
CHECK 01

Mechanism

Is the molecular story right? Ionotropic vs metabotropic, the G-protein (Gi / Gs / Gq) or channel, location (pre- vs post-synaptic, auto- vs heteroreceptor), and direction of effect.

CHECK 02

Affinity / pKi data

Do the binding values and the agonist / antagonist / partial labels in the affinity plate match a real source? Confirm relative potencies and that the radar / matrix encodes them correctly.

CHECK 03

Clinical claim

Does receptor → action → presentation hold? Cross-check the mapping against Stahl Table 2-4 (receptors) / 2-5 (transporters) and the clinical chapters.

CHECK 04

Citation

Does the cited paper actually support the claim, and does the PMID resolve to that paper? Set the citation status — verified, provided, conflicting, or needs-source.

Conservator’s opening findings

Two issues were already surfaced while assembling this desk — treat them as the first entries on your fix-list:

Muscarinic M1 & M3 citation — wrong PMID. The atlas cites PMID 24445063 for Kruse et al. (2014) Muscarinic acetylcholine receptors. That PMID resolves to an unrelated geriatrics paper. The correct identifier for that DOI (10.1038/nrd4295) is PMID 24903776. Both rows are pre-flagged conflicting.

Three specimens have no peer-reviewed source yet. D3, DAT, and 5-HT3 appear in the Archive without a dedicated citation. Each is pre-flagged needs-source with a one-click PubMed search to start from.

Eight Cabinet citations are marked provided — present in the atlas but not yet independently re-verified here. Eleven were confirmed against PubMed and are marked verified.
