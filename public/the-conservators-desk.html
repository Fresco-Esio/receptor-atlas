How this works

Nothing goes on the wall without a paper behind it. That is the whole job. Every specimen in the atlas should rest on at least one source you have actually read, and this desk exists to get each one there and keep it there.

The loop

  1. Pick a specimen from the queue. Filter to Unsourced to see the ones with nothing behind them at all; those are the real backlog.
  2. Read the ledger in the rail beside the content. Those papers back the claim, the narrative, and the clinical row. If it is empty the specimen says so in vermilion, and there is a PubMed search seeded with the receptor's own terms.
  3. Attach a source. Type an author, a title, or a PMID: matches from the shared library appear as you type, or create a new record inline. Edit a paper once in the library and every specimen citing it updates.
  4. Set each source's status as you check it. Then work the content below, edit anything that is wrong, and tick the four checks in the review card.

Every edit saves to the atlas database as you type, and the three volumes read from that same record. There is no publish step.

What the marks mean

Unsourced. Nothing is attached at all. This is the one state the desk exists to eliminate, so it carries the accent colour.
Provided. Carried in from the atlas, not yet re-checked by you.
Verified. You opened the source, it supports the claim, and its identifier resolves.
Conflicting. A source is wrong or disputed. One of these flags the whole specimen.

Two levels of source

The database keys a source either to the specimen or to a single binding, so the desk shows them in those two places and nowhere else. The ledger in the rail backs the specimen's claim, narrative and clinical row. A binding's Ki carries its own citation on its own row, because a number needs the paper that measured that number.

Sourcing the binding numbers in bulk

Most affinities come from one database, so verifying that record once in the Source library clears every binding citing it. Open the library, find the source, and set its status there.
